Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 29, 2026 • Effective immediately for all users

1. Introduction

Welcome to Easy & Fast Earn ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data. This Privacy Policy informs you about how we handle, collect, and protect information when you visit or interact with our web application, Telegram bot, and related services.

By accessing or using Easy & Fast Earn, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to deliver transparent rewards and anti-abuse protection:

  • Telegram User Identification: Telegram User ID, first name, last name, and username provided via Telegram WebApp secure authentication to uniquely associate your wallet ledger and referral progress.
  • Transaction & Ledger Records: Balances, withdrawal requests, payment destination details (e.g., UPI ID, PayPal email address, bank account number), and audit timestamps.
  • Activity Data: Daily check-in timestamps, verified channel subscriptions, partner task completions, and rewarded advertising session tokens.
  • Device & Log Information: Standard server logs, IP address, browser type, and operating system collected for security, anti-fraud enforcement, and server health monitoring.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We partner with Google AdSense and authorized digital ad platforms to serve ads on our site:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on Easy & Fast Earn.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • We also work with Monetag and GigaPub to deliver verified video and interactive partner sponsorships.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Easy & Fast Earn uses cookies to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our rewards platform.
  • To process and disburse verified withdrawal payouts accurately.
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, unauthorized botting, and sybil multi-account attacks.
  • To comply with regulatory obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

6. Data Security & Retention

We adopt industry-standard data storage and encryption protocols to safeguard your records. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We retain information only for as long as necessary to fulfill reward fulfillment, tax/accounting requirements, and dispute resolutions.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Easy & Fast Earn does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such information from our records.
